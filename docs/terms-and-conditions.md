## 2. Roles & Platform Relationship
Paridhan operates as a marketplace platform and intermediary under India's Information Technology Act, 2000:
- **Consumers:** Purchase garments, negotiate prices via bargaining features, and place delivery orders.
- **Sellers:** Independent local businesses listing products, establishing base and minimum floor prices, and fulfilling orders.
- **Delivery Partners:** Independent logistics providers accepting pickup and delivery assignments.
- **Paridhan Marketplace:** Technology provider facilitating discovery, communication, and payment settlements.

## 3. Real-Time Bargaining Rules
- All bargain offers made through the platform are binding upon seller acceptance.
- Offers cannot be made below the seller-configured floor price (`min_bargain_price`). Any attempt to tamper with client checks or bypass server validation will lead to account suspension.
- Once an offer is accepted by either party, an order reservation / cart item is generated at the agreed price.

## 4. Payments and Split Settlements
- Online payments are processed securely via **Razorpay Route**. Platform commissions are automatically retained, and seller settlements are remitted according to the settlement cycle.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** The delivery partner collects the exact invoice amount upon delivery. Delivery partners are legally required to remit all collected COD funds promptly to the platform/seller.

## 5. User Conduct & Compliance
Users must not upload infringing, unlawful, counterfeit, or misleading apparel listings. Listings violating intellectual property or consumer protection norms will be removed immediately.
